## Named victim toast

BLOCKED. A guest troll does not say who sent it. `trolls.active` carries the effect id, kind, role, status, label, and escape. The sender's nickname is only on the admin `hostEffects` list, which the phone does not receive. The toast stays "You've been trolled" with no name. Do not add the sender to the guest payload for this.
