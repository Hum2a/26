## Freddo

- Current retailer listing: [Tesco, Cadbury Dairy Milk Freddo Chocolate Bar Single 18g](https://www.tesco.com/shop/en-GB/products/275341411), displayed price **£0.35** when checked. This is one dated retailer price, not a claim that all shops charge the same or an annual average.
- Historical timeline: [StepChange, 30th anniversary report, January 2023](https://www.stepchange.org/Portals/0/assets/About/30th-anniversary-report-january-2023-StepChange.pdf), PDF page 8, records the price rising to **20p in 2011**, then **25p in 2014**. Using 20p for 2012 is an inference from that timeline, not a verified Tesco transaction from 2012.
- Contemporaneous corroboration: [“the tax on freddos” parliamentary petition](https://petition.parliament.uk/archived/petitions/14571), published 1 September 2011 and closed 1 September 2012, complains about a **20p** Freddo. The petition is a member of the public's statement hosted by Parliament, not official government price statistics.
- The historical sources establish the typical single-bar price but do not establish an identical 18g Tesco SKU in 2012. The question therefore says “a standard UK Freddo was typically 20p”, and identifies Tesco/18g only for 2026. No claim about unchanged weight or retailer is made.

## Petrol

Source: Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, [Weekly road fuel prices](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/weekly-road-fuel-prices). The series describes UK retail pump averages; **ULSP** is unleaded petrol, not diesel. Values are pence per litre and include duty and VAT.

- [2003–2017 official table](https://www.gov.uk/csv-preview/68a3326b32d2c63f869343a3/weekly_road_fuel_prices_2003_to_2017.csv), row **15/10/2012**: ULSP **138.35**, diesel **143.23**. The question uses the ULSP column.
- [2018–2026 official table](https://www.gov.uk/csv-preview/6acd0403ec9d3aba1e013c7b/CSV__2018_-__.csv), row **12/10/2026**: ULSP **175.28**, diesel **200.04**. The question uses the ULSP column.
- The selected dates are comparable mid-October weeks. Neither value is presented as a full-year average. Future updates to the government tables do not silently change this versioned question.
- The stored scoring target is **175**, from rounding **175.28** to the nearest penny, so the ordinary one-penny slider can reach it. The exact published value appears in the reveal. The slider range is 50–300p; its initial value is the historical reference rounded to 138p. These are gameplay settings, not additional economic observations.

Both questions retain the ordinary slider defaults: 20 seconds, 100 points for the exact scoring answer and no speed bonus. Freddo uses a 5–100p range and starts at the 20p reference. No new scoring engine or guessed economic facts were added.

### Tesco: “The student tasting menu”

- Historical source: [MoneySavingExpert, 21 October 2022](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2022/10/tesco-meal-deal-price-hike/) reports Tesco's statement that the £3 price had held for most people since 2012. This is a retrospective retailer statement, not a receipt for one particular 2012 store.
- Current primary source: [Tesco's standard meal-deal offer](https://www.tesco.com/shop/en-GB/buylists/whoosh-meal-deals/all-3pound-meal-deal/all-3pound-meal-deal), checked 20 October 2026, specifies £3.85 with a Clubcard and £4.25 regular price. Its displayed validity is 12 October 2026 to 9 May 2027. The question explicitly asks for the Clubcard price, excluding the premium deal. Loyalty terms and eligible products have changed; this is a comparison of offers, not identical contents or a national food index.

### Greggs: “The Greggs gold standard”

- Historical baseline source: [The Guardian, 19 September 2025](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2025/sep/19/prices-greggs-sausage-roll-pret-meal-deal-food-inflation-retailers).
- Dated 2026 source: [LBC, 8 January 2026, updated 10 February](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/greggs-raises-prices-sausage-roll-lattes-5HjdQQp_2/) reports £1.35 in most shops. The question names this reporting date; it does not imply that every Greggs till has that price in October. London's higher prices, motorway outlets and delivery charges are excluded from the intended comparison.
- Greggs's [own sausage-roll product page](https://www.greggs.com/menu/product/sausage-roll-1000446/) identifies the product but supplies no nationwide retail price. The two historical observations are reported retail prices, not official averages or an identical-shop series.

### Maccies: “The 99p nostalgia tax”

- Historical primary source: [McDonald's, Our Pricing, 28 July 2022](https://www.mcdonalds.com/gb/en-gb/newsroom/article/our-pricing.html) announced the first cheeseburger increase in over 14 years, from 99p to £1.19. Applying the unchanged 99p period to 2012 is an inference from that statement, not a newly found 2012 menu.
- Dated 2026 source: [McDonald's Menu Prices UK, September 2026](https://mcdonalds-menu-prices.co.uk/mcdonalds-cheeseburger-price-uk/) lists a single cheeseburger at £1.39. **This is an independent fan guide, not McDonald's official price list or a verified local till quote.** Both phone wording and reveal identify it as a menu-guide price. Other guides and restaurants quote different amounts; the round asks for this specific published reference rather than asserting one national tariff.
- McDonald's [2024 £5 Meal Deal press release](https://www.mcdonalds.com/gb/en-gb/newsroom/article/p5-meal-deal-press-release.html) lists £1.39 for a cheeseburger and explicitly says individual restaurant prices vary. This supports the plausibility of the quoted amount but is **not** used as proof of the 2026 price. The round excludes meals, double cheeseburgers and delivery.

All three retain ordinary 20-second collection and 100 points for the exact answer. Broad bounds and starting at the historical price are gameplay choices. No new scoring bands were introduced.
