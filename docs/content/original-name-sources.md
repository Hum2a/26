# Original-name additions

[Home](../../README.md) · [Documentation](../README.md) · [Event runbook](../event-runbook.md)

Checked on 20 October 2026. The user requested two famous people with less obvious original names. Chosen as a judgement about recognisability and surprise, not a measured claim about how many guests know their names.

Both questions use the existing typed-answer mechanic and `redacted-file` presentation. The wording asks for an original name, rather than implying the celebrity's professional name is illegitimate or that a legal deed poll took place. No portrait asset is required. Ordinary defaults apply: 20 seconds, 100 points, 60-character entry, no speed bonus. First name plus surname is sufficient; middle names are optional, as stated to guests. The existing server matcher handles case, spacing, punctuation and small typos.

## `real-name-vin-diesel`

Canonical reveal: **Mark Sinclair**.

- Primary institutional source: [CUNY's 2018 commencement announcement](https://www.cuny.edu/news/cuny-to-graduate-thousands-of-students-in-commencement-ceremonies-with-leaders-artists-writers-and-more-as-keynote-speakers/) identifies Vin Diesel's given name as Mark Sinclair in its Hunter College section.
- Direct interview: [Industria, “I like to think of myself as a survivalist”](https://www.industriamovies.com/feature/like-think-survivalist-vin-diesel/) asks about his name as Mark Sinclair Vincent and his nickname. Accept **Mark Sinclair Vincent** as well, rather than penalising a guest who remembers this reported form.
- Original reporting: [TIME, Vin Diesel: Self-Help Guru, 26 March 2015](https://time.com/3759635/vin-diesel-self-help-guru/) records his earlier use of **Mark Vincent**, also accepted. The question deliberately says “original name”; it does not try to adjudicate which longer surname form belongs on a birth certificate.
- Do not accept just Mark, Sinclair, Vin or Diesel as complete answers. No current legal-name claim is made.

## `real-name-jamie-foxx`

Canonical reveal: **Eric Marlon Bishop**. Also accept **Eric Bishop**, with no middle name required.

- Biographical reference: [Biography.com, Jamie Foxx](https://www.biography.com/actors/jamie-foxx) supplies the full birth name.
- Direct interview: [The Irish Times, Charm skool](https://www.irishtimes.com/life-and-style/charm-skool-1.1005934) records his account of adopting Jamie Foxx while performing stand-up under the earlier name Eric Bishop. This supports the shorter accepted answer without demanding trivia about a middle name.
- [Tim Ferriss's original interview page](https://tim.blog/2015/12/06/jamie-foxx/) links an official transcript spelling the middle name “Marlin”. The general typo matcher accepts that one-character variant of Marlon; it is not a separate asserted birth name.
- Do not accept just Eric, Bishop, Jamie or Foxx as complete answers. No claim about present legal identity is needed.

The humorous reveal lines are original copy, not quotations. Private answers, accepted variants, source notes and explanations remain server-side until the existing reveal projection permits the answer. Admin phone rehearsals receive only the deliberate public question projection. Both questions are ready entries in the unreleased birthday catalogue and stay out of its running order.
