# Where are we? basemap

The round uses Leaflet 1.9.4 and a local GeoJSON dataset derived from [Natural Earth v5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson). The public-domain sources are the 1:110m admin-0 countries and simplified populated places. Keep the country geometries, country labels with label rank up to 4, and cities with scale rank up to 4. Coordinates are rounded to three decimal places and unused properties are removed. [Natural Earth terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
