# Kart: making it feel like a genuine race

[Current kart](kart-minigame.md) · [Evolution plan](kart-evolution-plan.md) · [Progress](build-progress.md)

Drafted 7 November 2026, on request: plan everything that would make the kart round feel like a real race, then build it.

**Status, 8 November 2026:** R1–R8 are built and tested. A real two-lap race ran end to end in the browser; see [build progress](build-progress.md). Physical phones, sound and slipstream tuning by feel are still to be checked.

## What is already there

The race already has authoritative arcade physics, counter-steer drifting with three turbo tiers, items, ordered checkpoints, recovery, a seeded grid, a 3-2-1-GO countdown, nearby opponents, a projector map and standings, and a recap. Those are kept.

## What is missing

Measured on the shared route data, a Park lap is about 22–30 s and a circuit lap about 50–70 s. Every race is one lap, so a race is one long time trial with other karts in it. Nobody sees a lap counter, a "final lap" moment, a place change or a lead change. Driving behind someone gives no advantage. The finish is a small card, and there is no sound.

## Plan

| ID  | Feature                                    | Where                                                                | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

| R1  | **Laps (1, 2 or 3)**                       | `packages/shared/src/kart.ts`, kart-only lobby, Admin course control | The host chooses laps beside the course in a kart-only lobby. The room-owned race question's window becomes the course window × laps, so slower guests still finish. The released birthday pack and its one-lap sprint do not change. ### Second pass (8 November 2026, on request)

| ID  | Feature           | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R9  | **Rocket start**  | A reaction start. The room times each kart's first throttle against GO: within 0.6 s is perfect (a Super boost for 1 s), within 1.2 s good (a Mini boost for 0.6 s), later is ordinary. Phones stay locked until the race opens, so no new pre-race input path was added; the windows allow for the phone seeing GO a little late. Only room races (`launchVersion: 1`) launch; CPU karts never do. The phone shows PERFECT START! or Good start!, and the projector calls the first perfect start.                  |
| R10 | **CPU racers**    | Up to seven, chosen beside the course and laps in a kart-only lobby. They start behind the guests and steer by aiming a little ahead on the course's own route, lifting for tight bends and holding their own top speed (skill 0.80–0.95). A stuck CPU kart returns to its last safe point. They skip item boxes but can be hit. They take places on the track and in the standings (tagged CPU), but points are shared by guest rank only, the race ends when every guest is done, and they never count as answers. |
| R11 | **Position luck** | Optional `luck: "position"` on the item rules, set by a host checkbox beside the item controls and off by default. Each box still picks deterministically, but weighted by standing: leaders mostly get oil, shields and tennis balls, and the back of the field mostly gets turbos, homing shots and bombs. Without it, the even rotation is unchanged.                                                                                                                                                             |

### Still not built

- **Kart-to-kart collisions:** the party decision is ghost karts ("nobody can blame traffic"). Slipstream is the one interaction between karts.
- **A held-throttle rocket start** (pressing during the countdown) would need inputs accepted before the race opens, a new authority path. The reaction start above covers the moment instead.

## Tests

- Shared rules: multi-lap counting, lap times and total, ranking across laps, recovery at the line, and slipstream charge, bonus and decay. Added to the `apps/api/test/kart-*.spec.ts` suites, next to the existing lap specs.
- Room: the lap choice is receipted, validated in the domain, replayed and frozen at start; the race window is scaled.
- Web: HUD, final lap, finish card, call-outs, the sound toggle and adapter, and the admin lap control sending the right command.
- Browser: a real multi-lap Park race on the dev server, plus a manual look in Chrome.
