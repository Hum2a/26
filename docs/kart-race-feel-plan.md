# Kart: making it feel like a genuine race

[Current kart](kart-minigame.md) · [Evolution plan](kart-evolution-plan.md) · [Progress](build-progress.md)

Drafted 7 November 2026, on request: plan everything that would make the kart round feel like a real race, then build it.

**Status, 8 November 2026:** R1–R8 are built and tested. A real two-lap race ran end to end in the browser; see [build progress](build-progress.md). Physical phones, sound and slipstream tuning by feel are still to be checked.

## What is already there

The race already has authoritative arcade physics, counter-steer drifting with three turbo tiers, items, ordered checkpoints, recovery, a seeded grid, a 3-2-1-GO countdown, nearby opponents, a projector map and standings, and a recap. Those are kept.

## What is missing

Measured on the shared route data, a Park lap is about 22–30 s and a circuit lap about 50–70 s. Every race is one lap, so a race is one long time trial with other karts in it. Nobody sees a lap counter, a "final lap" moment, a place change or a lead change. Driving behind someone gives no advantage. The finish is a small card, and there is no sound.

### Deliberately not in this pass

- **Kart-to-kart collisions:** the party decision is ghost karts ("nobody can blame traffic"). Slipstream adds racing between karts without bumping.
- **Rocket start:** phones are locked until GO, so a held-throttle start would need inputs accepted during the countdown, which is a new authority path. Recorded for later.
- **CPU racers and position-weighted items:** both change scoring fairness and the item rules. They need their own decision.

## Tests

- Shared rules: multi-lap counting, lap times and total, ranking across laps, recovery at the line, and slipstream charge, bonus and decay. Added to the `apps/api/test/kart-*.spec.ts` suites, next to the existing lap specs.
- Room: the lap choice is receipted, validated in the domain, replayed and frozen at start; the race window is scaled.
- Web: HUD, final lap, finish card, call-outs, the sound toggle and adapter, and the admin lap control sending the right command.
- Browser: a real multi-lap Park race on the dev server, plus a manual look in Chrome.
