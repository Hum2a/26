# Troll asset pack

The troll sounds and the stand-in Mum painting in `apps/web/public/assets/trolls/` come from Wikimedia Commons. Their licences differ file by file: CC BY-SA 3.0 and 4.0, CC BY 4.0, CC0 and public domain. The pack is not all CC0.

The full credits, source links, licence links and edits are in [`apps/web/public/assets/trolls/CREDITS.txt`](../../apps/web/public/assets/trolls/CREDITS.txt). That file ships with the site and is served at `/assets/trolls/CREDITS.txt`. The CC BY-SA adaptations (01, 04, 12 and 14) keep their source licence.

Only the files the game plays are shipped. The pack's originals, the alternate `02b` and `10b` clips and its preview montage are not.

The Netanyahu image and music, the glass smash and the Thomas theme sting in `apps/web/public/assets/` were supplied separately and are not part of this pack or its credits.
