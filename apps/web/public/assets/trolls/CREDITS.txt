Troll sound and image credits for 26.humza.website
==================================================

Files in this folder come from Wikimedia Commons, as listed in the asset
pack's SOURCES.md (licences as stated on each file page, fetched 7 November
2026). The licences differ file by file. The pack is NOT all CC0.

They are deliberate comic stand-ins. "Mum" is an old public domain painting and
an American English-lesson dialogue clip, not a real family member. The bubble
sound is a bubble pop, not bubble wrap. The AirDrop chime is a generic chime,
not an Apple sound. Nothing here is affiliated with Apple.

Changes made by the asset pack (before this project):
  Every MP3 was transcoded from the original to MP3, 44.1 kHz, 160 kbps, and
  loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS (12 was already an MP3 and was re-encoded).
  Further edits are noted per file below.
Changes made in this project: none. Files are used as supplied, under their
pack names. 02b, 10b, the originals and the preview montage are not shipped.

(01, 04, 12 and 14) are licensed under the same licence as their source,
