# Play

[Home](../../../../README.md) · [Documentation](../../../../docs/README.md) · [Event runbook](../../../../docs/event-runbook.md)

Phone and stage renderers for the public quiz snapshot. They do not score, and they do not know the answer until the snapshot includes a reveal. Shared answer widgets (send button, slider, option text, tiers) live in `controls/`. Kart and chess screens live in `apps/web/src/kart` and `apps/web/src/chess` and are lazy-loaded from `guest-play.tsx` / `stage-play.tsx`.

`guest-play.tsx` is the phone. `guest-slider.tsx` is the estimate control: a range input, lower and higher buttons, and an explicit send. Arrow keys move the range by its step. `guest-rank.tsx` places an item by tapping it and then tapping a position. `guest-text.tsx` sends one written line. `guest-ballot.tsx` lists ballot cards in the snapshot order inside a scrolling set of compact buttons, with a search field for guess-the-guest. `guest-duel.tsx` sends one number, or one finale prediction, with an explicit button. A pending number or contestant stays put when a new snapshot arrives. The phone states name the opponent, a solo target, a spectator, a late joiner, and a contestant waiting for predictions to close. `stage-play.tsx` is the projector. At the reveal it shows a slider's truth, guesses, and points, a ranking's order and points, at most eight vote or guess cards, or at most eight duel results. A longer list stays on the phones, with the full count and a note that scores include every duel. A longer ballot stays on the phones, with the full count on the projector. A host-featured set replaces that short list and does not change the tally. `copy.ts` is the phone wording. `attributions.tsx` shows a live name sample or the names attached at a multiple-choice reveal. Host controls, including a shortlist and a stage selection, stay on the admin screen. A hidden stage receives no question payload.

A phone can send one photo or a WAV voice clip while a question is running. After joining, the phone may send a browser and screen report for end-of-game statistics. A missing report does not block an answer. Eligible comparison cards reach the stage at the podium. The admin screen previews them earlier. A personal recap stays on that phone. Live typing, a voice troll, and a theme song use the same troll token and the stage cue queue. The stage enables sound with a click, and can mute or stop the current cue. The stage shows accepted media from the authorised route. Hiding it removes it from later stage and gallery requests. Guest trolls sit under the phone controls. The picker lists only effects the room offers, and only while the token is unlocked. An incoming invasion, call, or crack is a labelled dialog with a dismiss control. A dodging send button keeps the answer ids and can be stopped with Answer anyway. The stage shows one announcement caption at a time. The admin screen can cancel a running effect and dismiss that caption. Demo assets are text labels.
